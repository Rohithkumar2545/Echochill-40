# ECO CHILL 40 — LactroniX
*Smart Cooling. Fresh Milk. Better Livelihoods.*

## The problem
Small dairy farmers in hilly and North-Eastern India lose milk quality and income due to hours
of unchilled transport. 0.87 % of India's milk is lost post-harvest (MoFPI), largely due to the lack of
affordable, portable chilling for ~70 million small-scale milk producers.

## The solution
A 40-litre, electricity-free, passive milk chilling can using swappable ice-gel PCM cooling,
HDPE + PUF insulation, and low-cost IR/gas-sensor spoilage monitoring. Farmers exchange gel
packs daily at the village collection centre — no power needed at the farm.

## Validated results (10 L prototype)
- Cooling: 37.9 °C → 8.7 °C in 2 hours
- Hold: stable at 8.7 °C for 8+ hours
- Weight: ≈ 10 kg empty (vs ~15–20 kg for insulated SS alternatives)
- Cost: ₹5,000 (vs ₹7,000 for a conventional SS can)

## Novelty
First low-cost solution pairing swappable PCM cooling with a centralised daily-exchange logistics
model — a new rural cold-chain distribution pattern, not just a product.

## Scale-up plan
1. **Phase 1:** pilot with 1 dairy cooperative collection centre (50–100 farmers)
2. **Phase 2:** district-level rollout via NDDB-affiliated milk unions
3. **Phase 3:** state-level deployment across cooperative milk-collection networks

## Impact
- **Social:** improved livelihood security, quality awareness
- **Economic:** lower cold-chain expenditure, higher saleable milk value
- **Environmental:** reduced food waste, reusable components (SDG 2, 9, 12, 15)
