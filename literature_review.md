# Literature Review

## 1. Problem validation
**MoFPI / NABCONS (2022) — National Post-Harvest Loss Assessment.** Reports 0.87 % overall post-harvest milk loss
in India, citing transport delays, lack of temperature control during short-distance transport, and poor handling.
*Secondary:* "From Guava to Milk: What Food Loss Reveals About India's Supply Chains."
**Relevance:** establishes the time–temperature gap this can closes.

## 2. Thermal protection
**Xu et al. (2017), *Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering* — "Characterization of Insulation Performance of
Rigid Polyurethane Sandwich Panels."** Shows rigid PU (PUF) insulation effectively reduces heat transfer in cold-storage applications.
**Relevance:** supports PUF as the primary insulating layer.

## 3. Smart milk-quality monitoring
**Lima et al. (2018) — "Mastitis Detection and Prediction of Milk Composition Using Gas Sensor and Electrical Conductivity."**
Gas sensing (MQ-135-type) reached 77 % accuracy identifying milk-composition deviations.
**Relevance:** basis for gas-sensor spoilage-risk monitoring — a proxy indicator, not a bacterial count.

## 4. Passive thermal energy storage
**Huang, L. & Piontek, U. (2017), *Applied Sciences* — "Improving Performance of Cold-Chain Insulated Container with
Phase Change Material: An Experimental Investigation."** Insulation plus PCM substantially extends temperature-control duration.
**Relevance:** supports the wall + lid PCM cooling mechanism.

## 5. Field validation (primary data)
**LactroniX prototype testing (2026) — EcoChill-10 milk cooling validation log.** 37.9 °C → 8.7 °C in 2 h; stable hold at
8.7 °C for 8+ h. See [`prototype_validation.md`](prototype_validation.md).

## 6. Standards / specifications
**NDDB — Stainless-steel milk can specification (rev. May 2009):** 40 L SS304 can + lid weight 8.2–8.5 kg (baseline for weight comparison).
